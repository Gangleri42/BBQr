Why shares, not fountain codes

        

          UR fountain codes carry one message across a flaky camera link. Shares hand one secret to
          several holders. Different problem, different math.
        

        

          
            

              
              BBQr type M shares
              UR fountain codes
            

          
          
            
PurposeThreshold secret sharingErasure code for transport

            
Recovers fromAny k of n shares, in any orderEnough fragments of one stream

            
Below the thresholdNothing, information-theoreticallyEvery fragment carries plaintext chunks

            
Holdersn people or places, one series eachOne receiver watching one screen

            
IntegritySealed digest; corrupt shares are namedChecksum on the whole message

            
TransportAny BBQr encoding; 9 bytes overhead per shareIs the transport

          
        

        
A share has a file type of its own, so a decoder without the extension never shows it as the data.
